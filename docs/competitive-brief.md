# The contractor lead-market math, in one glance

A one-pager to attach to outreach emails or hand to a contractor in person. It
answers one question: **"Why should I pay DwellGauge instead of Angi / Thumbtack
/ my current lead source?"**

Keep it to one page. Print it, attach it as a PDF, or paste the table into an
email. This is sales material — the numbers below are the pitch.

---

## The headline

> **You are not paying for traffic. You are paying to control whether the
> homeowner gets straight to you — or has to filter through everyone else.**

Almost every home-services lead marketplace charges **per lead**, and the leads
they sell get shared, re-sold, and often weren't even a real request. DwellGauge
charges **one flat fee per month** and routes requests **direct to you**.

## Why the math matters

- Average Angi/Thumbtack lead: **~$90**.
- Two shared leads a month ≈ **$180** — and up to 3–4 competing contractors were
  also sold that same "leads".
- DwellGauge featured: **$99/mo flat** — roughly **two leads' worth of spend**,
  but every request routes **exclusively to you**.

> Most of our featured pros still run Angi or Thumbtack. They use it for volume
> and us for quality — then compare **cost per won job** after 30 days.

## Fair-market readings

These are public, well-documented ranges (Angi membership ~$288–300/yr plus
$15–85 per lead; Thumbtack ~$10–50 per lead, higher for big jobs; Fixr
$50–150 per lead). They are ballparks, not quotes from each platform. Reprint
them only as the ranges shown here, never as a specific current price for the
reader's market.

**Nothing on DwellGauge changes official record facts.** Payment buys placement
and routing priority — it never changes license status, source data, or excuses
a bad record.
